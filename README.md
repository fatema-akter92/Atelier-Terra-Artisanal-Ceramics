# Atelier Terra — Artisanal Ceramics & Cozy Home Living

-A high-aesthetic Japandi / Scandinavian ceramic e-commerce platform built with Python & Django.
- Inspired by warm morning light, hand-pleated paper lamps, tactile stoneware, and tranquil creative workspaces.

---

## ✦ Key Highlights & Unique Features

### 1. The Signature Hero Piece: "The Pleated Solstice Table Lamp"
- Based directly on the warm, inviting morning desk aesthetic: sharp-creased conical pleated shade on a powder-coated architectural iron tripod stand with soft 2700K ambient glow, paired with speckled Oregon stoneware bud vases and carved ceramic pencil cups.

### 2. Unique Feature 1: The Custom Ceramic Atelier (Glaze & Shape Configurator)
- **Live 2D/3D Canvas Pottery Visualizer**: Real-time rendering of ceramic silhouettes.
- **5 Base Silhouettes**: The Pleated Solstice Table Lamp, Sculptural Fluted Amphora, Wabi-Sabi Donut Moon Flask, Minimalist Desk Tool Vessel, Organic Tea Chawan.
- **Bespoke Glaze Recipes**: Speckled Oatmeal, Raw Fired Terracotta, Sage Celadon Mist, Warm Biscuit Sandstone, Matte Alabaster Cream, Midnight Basalt.
- **Artisan Base Inscription**: Custom maker's monogram or year stamped into the raw clay underside.
- **Stand Finish Selector**: Architectural White, Brushed Brass, or Matte Iron wire stand.
- **One-Click Commission**: Saves the custom configuration, calculates pricing, and adds the bespoke piece directly to the shopping basket.

### 3. Unique Feature 2: "Style My Cozy Nook" Virtual Room Styler
- **Visual Room Canvas**: Mirrors the morning study aesthetic with an off-white painted brick wall and natural honey oak desk surface with soft window light.
- **Interactive Decor Wardrobe**: Place and swap lamps, vases, desk organizers, wallmates, and tableware.
- **Live Harmony Meter**: Calculates aesthetic harmony percentages as pieces interact.
- **Automatic 15% Curated Bundle Discount**: Adds all styled room items into the cart with promotional code  automatically applied.

### 4. Atmospheric Experience: Warm 2700K Evening Glow & Calming Kiln Sound
- **Ambient 2700K Glow Mode**: Seamless top-bar toggle that shifts the entire website into the warm golden illumination of the pleated lamp.
- **Web Audio Meditative Soundscape**: Calming pottery wheel & warm kiln breeze sound synthesizer with gentle ceramic chimes.

### 5. Complete E-Commerce Lifecycle
- **Session Shopping Basket**: Supports both standard catalog products and custom-made atelier commissions.
- **Promo Discount System**: `WARMTH10` (10% off), `COZYNOOK15` (15% off curated room looks).
- **Checkout & Fragile White-Glove Courier**: Delivery choices with biodegradable honeycomb cushioning notes.
- **Kiln Progress Tracking**: Track order status across 5 milestones (*Order Received → 1280°C Kiln Firing → Quality Inspected → Dispatched → Delivered*).
- **Collector Accounts & Wishlists**: Order history, delivery addresses, and saved collections.
- **Collector Reviews**: 5-star rating system with verified purchase badges.
- **Comprehensive Django Admin**: Full management of products, images, glazes, orders, reviews, and subscribers.

---

## ✦ Technology Stack
- **Backend**: Python 3.12, Django 6.1
- **Database**: SQLite3 (pre-seeded with rich data, zero database setup required)
- **Image Processing**: Pillow 12.3
- **Frontend**: Django Templates, HTML5 Canvas, Vanilla ES6 JavaScript (Audio API & Visualizer), Tailwind CSS & Custom CSS (`atelier.css`)
- **Typography**: *Cormorant Garamond* (Headings) & *Plus Jakarta Sans* (Body)
- **Icons**: FontAwesome 6
